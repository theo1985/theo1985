### Theofanis Pantelides

Theofanis Pantelides is the chief executive of a federally regulated brokerage
in Manhattan. He was born in Nicosia, Cyprus, dropped out to build things, and
reads fine print for a living. He lives in Pennsylvania with his wife, two
children, and Mochi, who has never once followed a rule. This is his first book.

#### The book

**[Consequences May Vary: Past Obedience Is No Guarantee of Future Results](https://ctml-trust.com/)**
(CTML Trust, September 2026). Its claim fits in one sentence, and it gets
misheard every time it is said out loud: there are no rules, only consequences.
That is not permission. Twenty-three chapters, five interludes, one warning
label, and the instruments to audit the fine print of your own life.

Chapter twenty is about my own industry, and about the product being built next
door to it. Event contracts are markets where you buy yes or no on an election,
a temperature, a sentence being said out loud. You can be right eighty percent
of the time and still lose everything, if you are paying eighty-one cents for
contracts that pay a dollar. Accuracy is not edge. Price is.

#### The code

I got to the glass room by writing code: web developer, startup co-founder,
trading-systems developer, product manager, CIO. I still write it. Most of it
lives in private repos: trading infrastructure, storefronts for small businesses
and a family farm, and a home system that archives the cameras and tells me when
a bear is in the yard.

What is public here is old, a 2014 iOS bootcamp and a few early experiments,
archived and kept for the record.

There is no appendix. There is only what happens next.
